# Website project brief

Use this before asking a developer for a quote. Fill in what you know, and leave anything uncertain blank.

## Goal
What should the website help people do?

## Audience
Who will use it? What do they need to find first?

## Pages and content
List pages you expect. Who will write the copy, provide images, and approve them?

## Design references
Share links you like and explain what works for you. Note any brand colours or accessibility needs.

## Functions and integrations
Forms, payments, booking, logins, analytics, or other systems? Which accounts already exist?

## Operations
Who will update the site? Who will own the domain and hosting account?

## Timeline and budget
What date matters, and why? State a budget range if you have one.

## Success and handover
What result would tell you the project worked? What access and documentation will you need afterward?
