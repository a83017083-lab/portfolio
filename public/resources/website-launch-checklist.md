# Website launch checklist

A starting checklist, not a guarantee that a site is ready for every business or law.

- [ ] Confirm the final domain, account owner and hosting access.
- [ ] Check names, contact details, pricing and claims against approved copy.
- [ ] Test core journeys on mobile and desktop, including form errors and confirmations.
- [ ] Check keyboard navigation, focus, alt text and readable contrast.
- [ ] Open navigation and outbound links; check broken and missing pages.
- [ ] Confirm privacy and consent text match what the site actually collects.
- [ ] Verify analytics and search indexing settings without exposing private pages.
- [ ] Back up content and confirm who can restore it.
- [ ] Check email delivery, spam handling and notification ownership.
- [ ] Make a rollback plan and confirm who handles launch-day issues.
